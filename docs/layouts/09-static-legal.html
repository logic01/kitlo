1. Eligibility & account

To use Kitlo, you must be at least 18 years old, a US resident, and able to enter binding contracts. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

2. Listing gear on Kitlo

Listers must complete identity verification and connect a payout account before publishing. You retain ownership of your gear at all times. Kitlo never takes possession of listed items.

2.1 Listing accuracy

You agree that all photos, specifications, condition ratings, and descriptions you submit are true and accurate to the best of your knowledge.

3. Booking & rental terms

A booking creates a binding rental contract between renter and lister. Kitlo is the payment facilitator and dispute mediator, but is not a party to the rental contract itself.

4. Payment, fees & refunds

Renters pay Kitlo at the time of booking confirmation. Kitlo charges a 5% service fee to the renter and deducts a 5% payout fee from the lister payout (Phase 1 launch pricing). Refunds follow the cancellation policy in Section 4.3.

5. Deposits & damage

Every booking includes a deposit hold authorized at booking confirmation. Deposits are released to the renter 48 hours after return confirmation, unless the lister files a damage claim within that window.

Lister Agreement

Plain-English terms for listing gear on Kitlo. The same agreement is shown inline during lister onboarding — this page is a permanent reference for active listers.

Kitlo take rate
5%
Deducted from each rental before payout. Renters separately pay a 5% service fee.

1. Your gear, your responsibility

You own the gear. You set the daily rate, the deposit, the availability. You decide whether bookings are instant-book or require approval. Kitlo never takes possession of your equipment.

2. Funds & payouts

Renters pay Kitlo at booking confirmation. We hold funds in escrow until both parties confirm return. Earnings deposit to your bank account within 24 hours of return confirmation.

3. Damage & deposits

Every booking includes a deposit hold (you set the amount per listing). If gear comes back damaged, you have 48 hours from return confirmation to file a damage claim with photo evidence. Kitlo mediates between you and the renter.

4. Disputes

If you and the renter can't agree on a damage outcome, late fee, or other issue, Kitlo Support reviews evidence (photos, message thread, booking timeline) and issues a binding ruling within 5 business days.

5. Cancellation

You can cancel a confirmed booking, but lister-initiated cancellations within 48 hours of pickup may result in a $25 disruption fee to cover the renter's hassle. Repeat cancellations within a 30-day window will lower your rank in search.

6. Tax reporting

Kitlo issues a 1099-K once you cross the IRS reporting threshold for the calendar year. You handle your own taxes — Kitlo doesn't withhold.

7. Termination

Either party can end this agreement at any time. Open bookings must complete normally. Kitlo may also suspend or terminate listings that violate the Terms of Service.

8. Insurance

Kitlo's optional Protection Plan (paid by the renter at booking) covers up to $5,000 in damage per booking. For higher-value gear, you may carry your own coverage. Kitlo is not a primary insurer.

Questions? Email listers@kitlo.com.
