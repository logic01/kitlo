How Kitlo works

Rent smarter.
Hunt better.

Everything you need to know about renting and listing hunting equipment on Kitlo.

Find gear List your gear

For renters

How to rent gear

01

Create a free account

Sign up with email or Google. Set your location to see nearby gear. No verification required to browse.

02

Browse and request

Filter by gear type, condition, and dates. Read specs and reviews. Request the gear — no payment yet.

03

Pay securely

Your card is charged only after the lister confirms. Funds held in escrow until both parties confirm return.

04

Coordinate pickup

Message the lister through the platform. Full address revealed only after booking is confirmed.

05

Inspect at handoff

Review the gear on pickup. Flag any condition discrepancies before confirming — your protection window.

06

Return and review

Confirm return in the app. Leave an honest review — your feedback helps the community.

For listers

How to earn from your gear

01

Verify your identity

Government ID + selfie via Stripe Identity. One time only. Required before your first listing goes live.

02

Connect your bank

Link your payout account via Stripe Connect. Secure and bank-level encrypted.

03

Create a listing

Add 3+ photos, specs, condition, and daily rate. Listings priced under $500/day publish instantly.

04

Accept bookings

Review renter profiles and confirm or decline requests. Or enable Instant Book to skip the review.

05

Hand off the gear

Meet the renter at the agreed pickup location. Confirm handoff in the app to start the rental clock.

06

Get paid

Funds released 1 business day after confirmed return. Deposited directly to your bank account.

Condition rating system

Honest gear conditions

Mint

Looks and performs like new. Zero field wear. Often still has original packaging or accessories.

Field-Ready

Moderate use, fully functional. Minor cosmetic marks. Maintained and ready to hunt without hesitation.

Battle-Scarred

Heavy use. Cosmetic wear visible. All specs and functions are fully intact — a workhorse, not a showpiece.
