Lister agreement

A plain-English read. The full legal version is linked at the bottom.

Kitlo take rate

Deducted from each rental before payout

15%

1. Your gear, your responsibility

You are the owner. You set the rate. You decide who rents it (with instant book or manual approval).

2. Funds & payouts

Renters pay Kitlo. We hold funds until both parties confirm return. Earnings deposit to your bank within 24 hours after that.

3. Damage & deposits

Every booking includes a deposit hold (set per listing). If gear comes back damaged, you can flag it within 48 hours. Kitlo mediates.

4. Disputes

If you and the renter can't agree, Kitlo Support reviews evidence and makes a binding ruling within 5 business days.

5. Cancellation

You can cancel a confirmed booking, but repeat lister-initiated cancellations within 30 days lower your rank in search.

6. Tax reporting

Kitlo issues a 1099-K once you cross the IRS reporting threshold for the calendar year. You handle your own taxes.

7. Termination

Either party can end this agreement at any time. Open bookings must complete normally.
