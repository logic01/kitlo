# Kitlo — Gear Catalogue & Listing Policy

> Which gear belongs on the platform, which doesn't, and why.

Sourced from research conducted April 2026. Rent vs. own verdicts are based on four signals:
**unit cost** (high = rent appeal), **use frequency** (infrequent = rent appeal), **portability** (hard to travel with = rent appeal), **identity attachment** (becomes part of someone's kit = own preference).

**Listing Key:** ✅ Accept — strong rent candidate | ⚠️ Accept with caveats | ❌ Decline — own-preference item

---

## Night Hunting Optics

The flagship vertical. Thermal and night vision are complementary technologies used sequentially on the same hunt — thermal detects (heat signatures at distance), NV engages (visual detail for shot placement). The bundle rental covering both in one booking is the core product, with no equivalent offered by any B2C or P2P competitor today.

**Catalogue purity: Very High** — nearly every item at a meaningful price point is rent-favorable. Almost no own-preference noise at the price levels that matter.

### Core Product

The **bundle rental** — thermal monocular for detection + NV scope for engagement — covers a complete night hunt in one booking. Avg booking value: **$500–900 for a 3–5 day hunt**. No B2C or P2P competitor offers this combined product today. This is the flagship listing type Kitlo should actively promote and optimize for.

## Pricing Reference

Pulled from active B2C rental operators (April 2026 research). Use these as market rate anchors for suggested list prices.

| Operator | Gear | 3-Day Rate | TBYB Incentive |
|----------|------|------------|----------------|
| Ultimate Night Vision (ships nationwide) | ATN ThOR thermal, PVS-14 NVG, full catalogue | $199 (ATN ThOR); $200–500/weekend NVG | 100% of rental fee applies toward purchase |
| Feral Texas Outdoors (Holland, TX — local + ships) | AGM Rattler, Nocpix, RIX Storm, InfiRay, ObservIR | $120–$375 scopes; $150–$350 handhelds | Rental fee waived if unit purchased within 15 days |
| optics4rent.com (ships nationwide) | Swarovski and premium daytime spotters | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |

**Key pricing insight:** Rent-aversion disappears when the gear is expensive enough. A hunter balked at $83/14-day for a $250 rangefinder (33% of purchase price). The same hunter accepts $199/weekend for a $5,000 thermal scope (4% of purchase price). Focus the catalogue on high-value units — the price-ratio math works in Kitlo's favor.

## Community & Supply Acquisition

The hunting optics rental conversation lives on specialized forums — not Reddit. Target supply-side outreach at:
- **HuntTalk** (38,657 members, 2.2M messages)
- **Airgunnation** (confirmed active renters; used and recommended mail-order services)
- **Sniper's Hide**
- **Texas Hunting Forum** (primary market: TX hog hunting is year-round)
- **Rokslide**
- State-level hunting Facebook groups (TX, AL, MS, WI, MN)

**Trust note:** The hunting community is trust-insular. Cold outreach converts poorly. Warm introductions through hunting content creators (YouTube, podcasts) and partnering with Texas hunting ranches/lodges will outperform any paid social campaign for supply acquisition.
