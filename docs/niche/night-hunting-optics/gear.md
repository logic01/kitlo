# Night Hunting Optics — Gear

Phase 1 vertical. Flagship product is the **thermal-detect + NV-engage bundle**. Only optics that mount to a renter's own weapon — the platform never moves the weapon itself.

**Weapons prohibited.** No firearms, ammunition, firearm components, bows, crossbows, or arrows. ITAR-allowed civilian-export thermal/NV only.
