# Ice Fishing — Gear

Phase 2 layered vertical (new). Seasonal winter extension on the overlanding owner base. Cold-weather electronics and powered tools dominate the high-AOV catalogue. Filter for **specialty, high-value items** ($200+ retail typical) where the rental ratio clears 5–15%. Big-box Frabill / Walmart entry gear is decline — buy-use-resell beats rental at that price.

## Listing Requirements Specific to this Vertical (proposed)

- **State regulations attestation** at booking — renter confirms current state ice-fishing license, decoy/spearing legality, and any limits on motorized augers (regs vary by state and lake).
- **Ice-condition disclaimer** at handoff — owner not liable for ice safety; renter responsible for ice-thickness assessment.
- **Propane safety attestation** — bulk tanks delivered empty (per `Prohibited`); CO detector strongly recommended in any enclosed shelter rental.
- **Wheelhouse caveat** — Kitlo lists the *shelter*, not a titled/registered RV. Wheelhouses on titled trailers require tow-vehicle attestation and may sit at the edge of the `Vehicles` prohibition; review on first listing.
- **Battery cold-rating declaration** — LFP batteries listed with rated cold-weather discharge spec; lead-acid units permitted but flagged as lower performance.
