## Listing Requirements (Vertical-Specific)
- Wading boot sole material declared — felt vs. rubber. Cross-state booking defaults to rubber.
- Between-rental decontamination attestation — didymo, whirling disease, NZ mudsnail protocol at handoff and return.
- State fishing-license attestation at booking — Kitlo does not sell licenses.
- Owner-as-guide intel field — optional free-text for hatch / access notes.
- Drift boats and trailers require vehicle-fit / tow-rating info (mirrors overlanding listing rules).
