How it works
Three steps.

02

Deposit held on card. Releases to lister at confirmed pickup. Renter Protection Plan covers damage up to $5,000 — required on all bookings over $1,000.

Both parties confirm return in the app. Deposit released. Ratings left on both sides. Everything tracked in your phone from start to finish.
